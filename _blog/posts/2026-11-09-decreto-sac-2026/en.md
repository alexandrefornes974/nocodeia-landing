# Brazil Customer Service Decree 2026: AI and a Human Option

Brazil's 2026 customer service decree (Decree No. 13.135, the "SAC decree", from Serviço de Atendimento ao Consumidor) lets companies serve customers with artificial intelligence, as long as the customer can opt to talk to a person in real time. It covers suppliers regulated by the federal Executive and digital financial services. For ordinary retail, check with your legal team.

If your support inbox is packed with repeated questions and you have thought about adding an AI agent to answer them, here is the good news of the week: the rule provides for that path. This text shows what the [decree](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_ato2023-2026/2026/decreto/d13135.htm) says (in Portuguese), who it reaches and how a [custom AI agent](/en/#servicos) handles the simple requests and calls a person at the right moment. The decree quotes below are our own translation of the Portuguese text.

## What does Brazil's 2026 SAC decree say about serving customers with artificial intelligence?

The decree allows AI in customer service, with one condition: the customer must have the option to talk to a person in real time. It is in art. 3, paragraph 3, which says the supplier "may adopt automated procedures, including artificial intelligence systems" (our translation) to screen and resolve requests.

In practice, the agent can greet the customer, understand the request and resolve whatever is simple. A human stays available for the rest.

Two other rules matter if you serve customers by chat. Art. 12 asks for at least one electronic channel working 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. And paragraph 1 of the same article guarantees real-time service by a human being "from eight a.m. to eight p.m., uninterruptedly, seven days a week" (our translation).

The design the rule opens up: the channel runs 24 hours, a person is available from 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. every day, and the agent answers up front, including in the hours outside that window.

## Does the decree apply to your company?

It depends on your sector. Art. 1, sole paragraph, lists two groups: suppliers of products and services regulated by the federal Executive branch, under the criteria of each sector's regulator, and suppliers that offer financial services through digital means, subject to the Central Bank of Brazil.

According to [Mercado&Consumo](https://mercadoeconsumo.com.br/30/10/2026/direitos-do-consumidor/novas-regras-do-sac-o-que-muda-e-quais-empresas-precisam-se-adaptar/) (in Portuguese), physical stores and e-commerce selling ordinary products are not automatically included. In other words, a bakery or a clothing store may be outside the decree, unless they have a regulated activity or offer a financial service.

Even so, the rule works as a reference. In our assessment, support that resolves fast and keeps a person one tap away works well in any sector.

To find out whether your business is covered, check with your legal team or your regulator. This text explains the decree and does not replace that check.

## What applies to chat and WhatsApp, and what applies only to phone?

The waiting-time limits that the press highlighted apply to the phone. For chat and messaging, the decree deals with the electronic channel, under different rules. The table below separates the two.

| Channel | What the decree provides | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Electronic (chat, messaging) | Channel working 24 hours a day, 7 days a week | Art. 12, main text |
| Electronic (chat, messaging) | Human in real time from 8 a.m. to 8 p.m., 7 days a week | Art. 12, paragraph 1 |
| Electronic (chat, messaging) | AI allowed, with the option of a human in real time | Art. 3, paragraph 3 |
| Phone | Human from 8 a.m. to 8 p.m., 7 days a week | Art. 14, paragraph 1 |
| Phone | Wait of up to 20 seconds for at least 80% of requests and up to 300 seconds (5 minutes) for the rest | Art. 15, item I |

[Tecnoblog](https://tecnoblog.net/noticias/novas-regras-de-sac-entram-em-vigor-no-brasil-veja-o-que-muda/) (in Portuguese) summarizes the phone rules. The decree also says, in art. 15, paragraph 1, that the maximum waiting times (20 and 300 seconds) do not apply when the sector's regulator has a specific rule. Sectors with their own rules may have different deadlines.

On the date: art. 25 says the decree takes effect twenty days after publication. Published on October 13, 2026, the count lands on November 2.

## What does an AI agent that serves well and keeps a human one tap away look like?

A good agent resolves the simple, says when it does not know and hands the customer to a person without making them repeat everything. Four details make a difference day to day:

1. **A visible option to talk to a person.** The customer should not have to guess the magic word. A button or a clear sentence does the job.
2. **An agent focused on the simple.** Order status, catalog, shipping, a reissued invoice, opening hours. These are the questions that repeat the most.
3. **A summary at handoff.** When the human takes over, they receive a summary of the conversation. The customer does not tell the story twice.
4. **Clear hours.** The agent says when a human agent is available and what happens outside those hours.

An AI agent, here, is an assistant that carries out tasks beyond answering messages. It checks the catalog, confirms shipping and logs the order. When the conversation leaves the script, it calls the team.

## How does Nocodeia handle the handoff to a human on Amora?

On Amora, the agent answers and a person steps in when needed. Amora is the customer service agent of Amore Totale, an online store, and it is a Nocodeia case. It serves customers on the website 24 hours a day and on Instagram and Facebook, in a conversation hub (Chatwoot), and checks the catalog, shipping and the blog to answer correctly.

When a conversation needs a person, the team receives an alert on Telegram with a summary of the conversation. That is the handoff to a human: the agent resolves what it can and passes on the rest, already with context.

Nocodeia's measurement over the first months showed that 80% of conversations were resolved by Amora without a human, a number authorized by the client. See the full [Amore Totale and Amora case](/en/cases/amore-totale/).

The logic is the one art. 3, paragraph 3 describes: AI up front, a person available. We do not state whether the store is or is not under the decree, and Nocodeia does not give legal opinions. What we show is how to set up support like this.

## Where do you start if your company has no agent yet?

Start with the questions your team answers every day. Three steps are enough for a first version:

1. **List the 5 most repeated topics** in your support, looking at the latest real conversations.
2. **Define when the agent calls a person**: a question outside the list, an upset customer or a request to talk to someone.
3. **Test with real conversations** before opening it to everyone, and adjust the answers.

## FAQ

### Does Brazil's SAC decree apply to chatbots?

The decree allows automated systems, including AI, as long as the consumer has the option of real-time service by a human being (art. 3, paragraph 3). In other words, automated service is possible, with a person available.

### Does the new SAC decree apply to every company?

No. Art. 1 covers suppliers regulated by the federal Executive, under the regulator's criteria, and digital financial services subject to the Central Bank of Brazil. According to Mercado&Consumo, ordinary retail is not automatically included, and the question is settled with your legal team.

### When does the SAC decree take effect?

Art. 25 says the decree takes effect twenty days after publication. With publication on October 13, 2026, the count lands on November 2, 2026.
