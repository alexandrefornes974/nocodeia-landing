Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026Controller: Nocodeia

This page explains how nocodeiaww.com, including the blog, handles visitor data. The full policy, which also covers an internal Nocodeia app, is available in Portuguese.

1. This website: form, cookies, statistics and ads

  • "I want my spot" form: the email you type is not stored on the site. It is placed in a WhatsApp message to Nocodeia, which is only sent if you press "send" in WhatsApp.
  • Visit statistics (Google Analytics 4): we use it to know how many people visit the site and which pages interest them most. Statistics cookies (_ga and _ga_*) are only set if you accept them in the cookie banner. Without your consent, Google may receive cookieless signals that do not identify you. We do not use this data for advertising.
  • Page behavior (Microsoft Clarity): with your consent in the cookie banner, we use Microsoft Clarity to understand how people browse the site, with heatmaps (where people click) and browsing recordings. The data goes to Microsoft.
  • Site performance and errors (Better Stack): with your consent in the cookie banner, we use Better Stack to measure site speed, find technical errors and view browsing recordings (clicks, scrolling and movements). What you type in forms is not recorded. Your IP address and approximate location (city) are also logged. Better Stack sets cookies in your browser, and the data goes to Better Stack, Inc., in the United States. We do not use this data for advertising.
  • Ads (Meta Pixel and LinkedIn Insight Tag): only with your consent in the "Marketing" category of the cookie banner, the site sends browsing data to Meta (Facebook and Instagram), through the Meta Pixel, and to LinkedIn, through the LinkedIn Insight Tag, to measure and personalize ads. Each of these companies handles this data under its own privacy policy.
  • Your cookie choice is saved in your own browser. You can change your mind at any time using the icon in the corner of the screen.
  • Hosting: the site is delivered by Cloudflare, which processes technical connection data (such as the IP address) to deliver the pages and protect the site against attacks.
  • Providers outside Brazil: Google, Microsoft, Meta, LinkedIn, Better Stack and Cloudflare may process this data on servers outside Brazil, under their own terms.
  • Your rights (LGPD, Brazil's General Data Protection Law): you can request access to, correction or deletion of your data and withdraw your consent by writing to the email below.
