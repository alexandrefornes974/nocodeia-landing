Before you decide

Estimate. It doesn't include the AI model, hosting, building the agent or the provider's monthly fee (it varies by provider, so check). Above 250,000 utility messages or 500,000 authentication messages per month, Meta applies volume discounts that this calculator doesn't consider.

Rates for Brazil in Brazilian reais, in effect since October 1, 2026, checked on November 20, 2026. Meta can only change them on the first day of a quarter: check the official table before deciding. Other countries have their own rates. Sources: Meta's official pricing documentation (section "Rate cards and volume tiers") and the official WhatsApp Business pricing calculator.

How the math works

Customer message to the businessfree
  • Service messages = conversations × agent messages per conversation.
  • Charged messages = service messages − (1,000 × number of phone numbers), never below zero.
  • Service cost = charged messages × R$ 0.035.
  • Marketing = marketing messages × R$ 0.3217. Utility and authentication = quantity × R$ 0.035.
  • Monthly total = service + marketing + utility + authentication. Yearly total = monthly total × 12.

Each line is rounded to the cent and the total is the sum of the lines. In the example: 17,000 × R$ 0.035 = R$ 595.00 and 2,000 × R$ 0.3217 = R$ 643.40, which add up to R$ 1,238.40. The calculator runs in your browser and doesn't store or send the numbers you type.
